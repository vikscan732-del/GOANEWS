Dayanand Mandrekar Alleges Party Harassment

Senior BJP leader Dayanand Mandrekar resigned as the Chairman of Goa State SC and OBC Finance and Development Corporation, citing harassment from the party. He stated that his Diwali greeting posters were removed and his workers were threatened.

Congress and Goa Forward Begin Alliance Talks

The Congress party and Goa Forward Party have held an extensive meeting to discuss primary seat-sharing for the 2027 Goa Assembly elections. AICC Goa desk in-charge Manikrao Thakre described the preliminary discussions as positive.

Crime

Two Booked for Theft in Pernem

Pernem Police registered a case against two Kerala natives for allegedly misappropriating cash, gold ornaments, and other property worth ₹4.30 lakh from their employer. The incident took place on November 11.

Politics

RGP Nears Seat-Sharing Agreement with Congress

Revolutionary Goans Party MLA Viresh Borkar announced that seat-sharing discussions with Congress for the 2027 Goa Assembly elections are in the final stage. The RGP has already submitted its specific constituency demands to Congress.

Crime

Scooter Stolen from Palolem Hostel

Canacona Police registered a case against a Kerala man for allegedly stealing a scooter from a Palolem hostel. The stolen vehicle contained laptops, cash, foreign currency, and a passport belonging to a guest.

Politics

ST Community Threatens Ballot-Box Backlash

Goa's Scheduled Tribe leaders warned of a political backlash during the 2027 Assembly elections if political reservation is not granted. UTAA slammed the state government for failing to properly defend their constitutional rights in court.

Crime

Decomposed Body Found in Raia

The decomposed body of a 35-year-old man from Maina-Curtorim was discovered near Daman East in Raia. Maina-Curtorim Police have registered an unnatural death case following the discovery.

Crime

Goa Police Arrest 24 in Cyber Crackdown

During an interstate operation named 'Cyber Vigil 2.0', Goa Police arrested 24 individuals and detected 37 cybercrime cases involving ₹8.22 crore. Forty-three police teams were deployed across 16 states for the crackdown.

New Bengaluru–Madgaon Vande Bharat Express Launched

Chief Minister Pramod Sawant noted that the newly launched Vande Bharat Express connects India’s IT capital directly with its tourist capital. Regular services begin on November 15, replacing the previous Mangaluru–Madgaon service.

IFFI 2026 to Showcase Over 200 Films

The 57th International Film Festival of India will feature 159 international films from 86 countries and 76 Indian films under the theme 'Stories Without Borders'. The event runs from November 20 to 28 with over 7,000 registered delegates.
