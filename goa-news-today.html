Chief Minister Pramod Sawant has urged women facing domestic violence or trouble at home to contact the 181 helpline. He emphasized that the helpline is available for immediate contact.

Politics

Rohit Monserrate to lead North Goa planning authority

The Goa government has reconstituted the North Goa Planning and Development Authority with Panaji Mayor Rohit Monserrate as its chairman. The authority's board will also include several local MLAs and municipal councillors.

Tourism

Goa notifies new taxi and premium cab fares

The State Transport Authority has notified distance-based fares for self-employed tourist taxi operators at beach and star hotels. Fares start at ₹300 for the first 3 km in hatchbacks or sedans, and go up to ₹1,000 for premium cabs.

WRD announces ₹741-crore plan for Salaulim Irrigation Project

The Goa Water Resources Department has initiated a modernisation plan for the Salaulim Irrigation Project. The department has invited proposals to select a consultancy firm to prepare the Detailed Project Report.

Crime

Cyber police warning over fake GSSC job advertisement

The Cyber Crime Police Station has registered a case regarding a fake GSSC Group C recruitment notice circulating on social media. Authorities have cautioned job aspirants about the fraudulent posting.

Tender floated to operate Panaji Port Terminal and new marina

The Captain of Ports Department has invited a ₹27-crore PPP tender to operate and maintain the new terminal building in Panaji. The selected operator will also manage local jetties and build a new yacht marina.

The Goa government has proposed a uniform trade licence and permission fee framework with different ceilings for Category A and Category B panchayat areas. The state has invited feedback on these draft rules within 15 days.
