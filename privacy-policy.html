Privacy Policy

TVGoa · Last updated: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TVGoa ("TVGoa", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the TVGoa mobile application and the website tvgoa.online.

Important: TVGoa is designed primarily to provide access to Goa-focused television, news and related content. We do not require users to create an account to use the basic features of the app.

1. Information We Collect

TVGoa does not intentionally ask users to provide personal information such as their name, phone number, home address or government identification number merely to use the basic app features.

Depending on the version of the app and services enabled, technical information may be processed automatically by the device, hosting provider, advertising provider, analytics provider, or third-party content/streaming services. Such information can include device type, operating-system version, IP address, approximate region, app/browser information, crash information and basic usage information.

2. How We Use Information

Information that is available to us may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain TVGoa and tvgoa.online.
  • Provide television, news and other content requested by users.
  • Improve app performance, reliability and user experience.
  • Detect abuse, security problems, crashes or technical errors.
  • Understand general usage of the service where analytics are enabled.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

3. Internet Connection

TVGoa requires an internet connection for online content and streaming features. Your network provider may independently process information about your internet connection and usage under its own privacy policy.

4. Third-Party Services and Content

TVGoa may link to, embed, or provide access to content and services operated by third parties. Third-party services may have their own privacy policies and may collect information according to their own practices.

Examples may include video/streaming platforms, external websites, map services, analytics services, advertising services, hosting providers and other technical service providers.

When you open or use a third-party service from TVGoa, that third party's privacy policy and terms may apply. TVGoa does not control the privacy practices of independent third parties.

5. Advertising and Analytics

If advertising or analytics services are enabled in a particular version of TVGoa, those providers may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, diagnostics and usage information in accordance with their own policies and applicable settings.

If no advertising or analytics service is enabled in your version of the app, this section does not mean that such information is being collected by TVGoa.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The TVGoa website or third-party services linked from it may use cookies or similar technologies for functionality, security, analytics or advertising. You can manage cookies through your browser settings where supported.

7. Location Information

TVGoa does not need precise device location for its basic television and news features. If a future feature requests location access, the app will request the relevant permission and explain the purpose of that access where required.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information as a standalone product. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the website or app, such as hosting, analytics, advertising, security, streaming or technical service providers, where applicable.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal process, protect users, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect the rights and security of TVGoa and others.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures intended to protect information handled through TVGoa. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

TVGoa is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for the basic use of the service.

11. External Links

TVGoa may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security or policies of external websites. Users should review the privacy policy of any external service they visit.

12. Your Choices

You can stop using the TVGoa app or website at any time. You can also control certain permissions, cookies, advertising settings and other device-level settings through your Android device or browser, where those controls are available.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, website, services or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or TVGoa's privacy practices, please contact us through the TVGoa website:

https://tvgoa.online/

15. Disclaimer About Third-Party Content

TVGoa may provide links or access to third-party television, video, news or other online content. The availability and ownership of such content may be controlled by the respective third-party providers. Users should refer to the relevant provider for its content rights, terms and privacy practices.
